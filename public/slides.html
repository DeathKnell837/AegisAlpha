The Challenge in Contract Compliance

Slow & Costly Manual Auditing

Legal teams spend dozens of hours reviewing NDAs and commercial agreements clause-by-clause, delaying business operations and sales cycles.

Hidden Liabilities & Risks

Standard LLMs lack context and often miss hidden risks or loopholes unless evaluated from opposing party perspectives.

Lack of Audit Traceability

Traditional AI tools operate as a single black box without planning, critiquing, or visual collaboration trails.

LexAudit 03 / 05

3-Agent Pipeline Architecture

1. Planner Agent

Analyzes the contract type (e.g. NDA, MSA) and creates a target compliance plan of 18 specific legal checkpoints.

2. Executor Agent

Executes the checklist. Reviews clauses line-by-line, identifies gaps, logs findings, and assigns confidence ratings.

3. Reviewer Agent

Critiques findings, runs adversarial simulation, calculates financial exposure, and issues the final compliance verdict.

LexAudit 04 / 05

Key Innovations

🗡️ Devil's Advocate Mode

The Reviewer agent critiques the Executor's findings from the opposing party's view, identifying hidden loopholes and challenging findings.

📊 Confidence Heatmap

Visualizes confidence alignment between the Executor and Reviewer per checkpoint to highlight where human review is needed.

💰 Dollar Exposure Estimation

Estimates and aggregates potential financial liability ranges for critical vulnerabilities, presenting it in a global risk banner.
