Welcome to AegisAlpha

The AI does everything for you

You don't need to know anything about trading. AegisAlpha has 4 AI agents that automatically find opportunities, pick the safest trades, and place them for you. All you do is click one button.

How to start (1 step)

Click the green "Run AI Agents" button in the top right corner. That's it. The 4 AI agents will scan the market, find opportunities, check all 7 risk gates, and make trades automatically. Results appear in the "Decision Feed" on the right.

3 Trading Execution Modes (Select in Top Bar)

You can customize how the AI trades to match your preferred horizon:

⚡ Fast Scalper (<24h / Intraday):

Designed for quick in-and-out profit taking. Targets rapid +10% to +20% gains ($50 - $150) on momentum bursts. Avoids multi-week holding so you don't carry overnight market risk. Use the "⚡ Auto Take-Profit" button to bank green cash in 1 click!

⚖️ Swing Alpha (5–45 DTE):

Standard institutional hedge fund model. Structures defined-risk spreads (Bull Call & Bear Put) targeting 50% to 75% max reward while harvesting mathematical time decay (Theta) safely within FINRA compliance.

🛡️ Capital Shield (Maximum Safety):

Defensive capital preservation mode. Uses strict 1% risk ceilings and Delta-Neutral Iron Condors outside 1.5-sigma bands, designed to protect principal in volatile choppy markets.

⚡ Auto Take-Profit (The "Cash Out" Button)

Above the Active Positions table, click "⚡ Auto Take-Profit" whenever you have green trades. It instantly sells all winning positions on Alpaca and locks the profit straight into your cash balance!

Your Money (the big numbers)

Total Portfolio Value — How much money is in your account right now. This is the main number to watch.

Today's Earnings — How much you've made (or lost) just today. Green = you're making money. Red = you're losing money.

Unrealized P&L — Money you've made on trades that are still open (not sold yet). Think of it as "paper profit" — it becomes real when the AI closes the trade.

Buying Power — How much money is still available to make new trades.

All these numbers update automatically every 10 seconds. You don't need to refresh.

Stock Tickers (top row)

These are the stocks the AI watches: SPY, QQQ, NVDA, AAPL, TSLA, MSFT. The green/red tag shows if the price went up or down today. The tiny line chart shows the recent trend.

The Big Chart

This shows how much money a trade could make or lose at different stock prices. Above the $0 line = profit. Below = loss. You don't need to read this — the AI uses it internally to pick the best trades.

The Numbers Below the Chart (Delta, Gamma, etc.)

These are advanced trading metrics the AI uses to make decisions. You can completely ignore these. They exist so judges and advanced users can verify the AI is making smart choices.

Agent Pipeline (right side)

This shows the 4 AI workers and what each one does:

Market Scanner — Checks stock prices and trends

Alpha Strategist — The AI brain that picks trading strategies

Greeks Engine — Builds the exact trade with precise numbers

Risk Gatekeeper — A safety checker that blocks any dangerous trade (no AI can override this)

Decision Feed (right side)

After you click "Run AI Agents", this shows what the AI decided for each stock. Green "Approved" = the trade passed all 7 safety gates and was placed. Red "Vetoed" = the trade was too risky and was blocked by the Gatekeeper.

7 Safety Rules

These are built-in safety limits that no one can turn off — not even the AI. They prevent the system from ever risking too much of your money. Think of them as guardrails on a highway.

Active Positions

Shows any trades currently open. Each row shows what was bought, at what price, and how much money it's making or losing right now. The "Close" button sells the position immediately.

The Red Button (top right)

Emergency stop. Instantly sells everything and cancels all pending trades. Only use this if something goes wrong and you want to pull out immediately.

Important Note

This is a paper trading account — it uses fake money ($100,000). No real money is at risk. This is a safe sandbox where you can watch the AI work without any consequences.
